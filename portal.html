01 — Start here

Before the math,
an intuition

"A monkey typing at random for an infinite time would almost surely write every text — every theory, every truth. This model is one of those pages. Not the final page, not the truth — a possible shape of structure in the noise."

You don't need equations to enter this model. You need one distinction.

To exist is to simply be — with no space, no time, no relation to anything else. Like the speed of light, or the bare nature of an elementary particle: a limit, not a thing sitting in a place.

To be existing is to be in relation — to need a link to another reference in order to project and take a place in the observable universe. Matter, distance, duration: all of it lives here.

Our entire reality turns on the difference between what exists and what is existing. That difference has a measure — a relational radius. From there, the rest follows as a way of seeing: light is the first relation, not a magnitude; gravity is the slow erasing of differences; a dimension is simply room for information that did not fit before.

Everything below — the predictions, the code, the papers — is an attempt to take this single intuition seriously, and to let it be wrong out loud.

02 — What the model claims

Four claims
the model makes

"A dimension is a space for new information — information that did not fit within the previous frame and is stored in a tangential space."

The model proposes that physical reality does not inhabit a pre-existing space. Space itself emerges from primordial relations. Each relational projection generates a new orthogonal layer of structure — a new dimension.

AXIOM I ∅→ℝ

Dimensional emergence

Dimensions are not the stage of physics — they are its product. They emerge as spaces for information that cannot fit within the previous projection.

AXIOM II 𝒫(Ω)

Generative principle

A single primordial relation, acting recursively, generates the complete hierarchy of observed structures: photons, protons, atoms, cosmos.

AXIOM III ∇·g

Gravity = information reduction

Distance is information between particles. Gravity is the natural tendency of systems to reduce their informational differences down to the identity threshold.

AXIOM IV ħ

ħ as a relational ceiling

ħ not as a lower bound but as a relational ceiling — the value at which the system closes coherently. All physical structure emerges from the partition of this primordial reference.

04 — Open computational layer

ROSI — the model
you can run

ROSI — Relational Orientation System for Information — is the runnable arm of the model. It takes its arithmetic backbone, the Mersenne closure spectrum Mε = 2ᵉ − 1, and turns it into a small, transparent logical machine: ordered thresholds, a complete logic-gate set, and circuits you can actually execute and inspect.

Arithmetic backbone Mε = 2ᵉ − 1  →  M₁=1, M₂=3, M₃=7, M₄ = 15 = 3·5, M₅=31 …
First compositional closure at ε = 4 — the structural seat the model assigns to the proton.

It is offered in open mode, in three senses:

  • Open source — run it, read every line, fork it.
  • Open about its limits — ROSI organises structure; it does not yet derive the numerical parameters of the Standard Model. That boundary is stated plainly, not hidden.
  • Open invitation — break it, stress it, extend it. The filter is the criterion, not the source.
